# Business Performance Analysis — Key Insights

## Project Overview
This document summarizes findings from the illustrative retail sales dataset used in the Business Performance Analysis Dashboard.

## 1. Overall Business KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | 19,425 |
| Total Profit | 6,355 |
| Total Orders | 10 |
| Profit Margin | 32.73% |
| Average Order Value | 1,942.50 |

## 2. Regional Performance

- **South:** Highest revenue, at 6,100.
- **West:** Second-highest revenue, at 4,400.
- **North:** Revenue of 3,250.
- **East:** Revenue of 3,075.
- **Central:** Lowest revenue, at 2,600.

## 3. Product Category Performance

- **Electronics:** Highest category revenue, at 6,100.
- **Clothing:** Revenue of 4,300.
- **Sports:** Revenue of 3,515.
- **Home & Kitchen:** Revenue of 3,080.
- **Beauty:** Revenue of 2,430.

## 4. Monthly Revenue Trend

| Month | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| January 2025 | 6,600 |
| February 2025 | 2,600 |
| March 2025 | 7,150 |
| April 2025 | 3,075 |

March recorded the highest revenue in this sample, while February recorded the lowest.

## 5. Recommendations

- Investigate the factors contributing to higher sales in the South region.
- Review the product mix and profitability of the Electronics category.
- Examine monthly sales fluctuations to identify possible seasonal or promotional effects.
- Monitor revenue and profit together before making business decisions.

## Data Limitations

The dataset contains only 10 illustrative records created for learning and demonstration. These findings are not representative of a real company and should not be used for actual business decisions.
